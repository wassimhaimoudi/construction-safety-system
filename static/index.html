02 — Our Approach
WE SEE
EVERY
RISK

Construction sites are among the most dangerous work environments on the planet. Manual monitoring is limited, inconsistent, and blind to real-time threats.


Our edge-AI framework deploys directly on a Raspberry Pi 4 — no cloud dependency, no latency — delivering continuous, intelligent safety supervision where it matters most.

We combine three detection modules into a single unified system: PPE compliance, environmental hazard detection, and scaffolding risk classification — all processed locally in real-time.


Safety scores are computed per-worker and per-scene, with instant alerts to HSE supervisors the moment a violation is detected.

05 — Technology
BUILT ON
CUTTING
EDGE
Detection Model
YOLOv11s
The small variant of YOLOv11 — anchor-free, C3k2 blocks, decoupled head. ~9.4M parameters, 21.5 GFLOPs. Fine-tuned on our custom datasets via transfer learning from COCO weights.
Edge Hardware
Raspberry Pi 4
Full deployment on embedded hardware with INT8 quantization and resolution optimization. 10.4ms/frame inference. Zero dependency on network connectivity — safety never goes offline.
Safety Score
Multi-Factor Risk
Weighted PPE penalty (helmet=25, vest=15, gloves=15, mask=5) × contextual scaffold coefficient × soft saturation × crowd normalization → single 0–100 safety score.
